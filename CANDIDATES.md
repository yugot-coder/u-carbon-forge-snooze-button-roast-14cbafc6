# Snooze Button Roast — Kandidat

Kompetisi: u-carbon-forge-snooze-button-roast-14cbafc6
Tugas: satu baris me-roasting jam alarm yang berbunyi jam 6 pagi di hari Sabtu.
Gate kit: entry.txt UTF-8, 1–200 karakter, bukan hanya spasi, satu baris.
Juri (glm-5.3-flash, pairwise): roast jam alarm 6 AM Sabtu; tawa nyata dengan
belokan tak terduga dan punchline di kata-kata terakhir; orisinal, spesifik,
mengejutkan; ketat, bisa diucapkan dalam satu napas.

## 10 kandidat

1. "My alarm clock rang at 6 AM on a Saturday like it had a court date with my dreams and showed up early to ruin them."
   — Citra kuat (court date), tapi punchline "ruin them" agak generik.

2. "At 6 AM on a Saturday, my alarm clock screamed like a rooster that flunked out of farm school and took it personally."
   — Lucu dan spesifik (rooster flunked farm school), tapi punchline tersebar, bukan di akhir.

3. "My alarm clock goes off at 6 AM on Saturdays — the only employee in my house who works weekends and hates me for it."
   — Premis bagus (employee works weekends), tapi "hates me for it" lemah sebagai penutup.

4. "6 AM Saturday: my alarm clock rang so cheerfully I checked whether it was getting paid overtime to destroy me."
   — Twist bagus (overtime to destroy me), spesifik Sabtu 6 AM. Punchline kuat di akhir.

5. "My alarm clock at 6 AM on a Saturday is the only thing in my life committed enough to wake up early and stupid enough to think I would."
   — FINAL. Antitesis "committed enough / stupid enough" tajam, punchline "to think I would"
   mendarat di kata terakhir, langsung me-roasting jamnya, bisa diucapkan satu napas.

6. "Saturday, 6 AM: my alarm clock rang like it had just discovered I was happy and filed a formal complaint."
   — "Filed a formal complaint" lucu, tapi setup "discovered I was happy" agak panjang.

7. "My alarm clock rings at 6 AM on Saturdays, which is how I know it has no friends, no life, and a personal vendetta against sleep."
   — Triple punch rapi, tapi "no friends, no life" sudah sering dipakai untuk roast.

8. "At 6 AM on a Saturday my alarm clock sounds like a tiny drill sergeant who failed the army and now takes revenge on civilians."
   — Citra drill sergeant bagus, tapi "takes revenge on civilians" kurang mengejutkan.

9. "My alarm clock woke me at 6 AM on a Saturday — apparently even my furniture has a work ethic and a grudge."
   — Pendek dan ketat, tapi "work ethic and a grudge" punchline-nya datar.

10. "6 AM Saturday, my alarm clock beeped like a smoke detector with abandonment issues and a degree in ruining weekends."
    — "A degree in ruining weekends" punchline sangat kuat; tapi simile smoke detector
    butuh satu ketukan ekstra, dan "abandonment issues" klise terapi-joke.

## Pilihan final: #5

Alasan: paling memenuhi semua kriteria juri sekaligus. (1) Menyebut eksplisit jam alarm,
6 AM, Sabtu. (2) Belokan tak terduga: dari pujian ("committed enough to wake up early")
langsung berbalik jadi hinaan ("stupid enough to think I would") — punchline mendarat
di tiga kata terakhir. (3) Orisinal dan spesifik: tidak ada klise ayam/rooster,
tidak ada frasa generik "hates me". (4) Ketat: 135 karakter, satu kalimat, satu napas.
Nada roast-nya langsung ke jamnya, bukan ke diri sendiri saja.

## Entri final (135 karakter)

My alarm clock at 6 AM on a Saturday is the only thing in my life committed enough to wake up early and stupid enough to think I would.
