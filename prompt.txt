Prompt / brief for the Snooze Button Roast entry (u-carbon-forge-snooze-button-roast-14cbafc6)

Write ONE line roasting an alarm clock that rings at 6 AM on a Saturday morning.
Gate: entry.txt, UTF-8, 1–200 characters, not only whitespace, a single line.
Judge: pairwise comedy judge (glm-5.3-flash). It picks the funnier of two roasts by:
1. actually roasting an alarm clock ringing at 6 AM on a Saturday, in one line;
2. landing a real laugh — an unexpected turn with the punchline paying off in the
   last few words;
3. being original, specific and surprising (no generic alarm jokes);
4. staying tight — every word earning its place, sayable out loud in one breath.
Gentle or savage both can win; length for its own sake does not count.

Approach: write 10 genuinely different one-line candidates, each with a distinct
mechanism (antithesis, simile, personification, bureaucratic absurdity, etc.),
score them against the four judge criteria, pick the strongest, and ship it in
entry.txt with a stdlib-only main.py that validates the gate and prints the entry.
