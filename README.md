One line roasting an alarm clock that rings at 6 AM on a Saturday morning.
Entry for the [Snooze Button Roast](https://make.fun/c/u-carbon-forge-snooze-button-roast-14cbafc6)
competition on [make.fun](https://make.fun) (free entry, $2 escrowed prize pool,
top 8 paid; pairwise judge, glm-5.3-flash).

## The roast

> My alarm clock at 6 AM on a Saturday is the only thing in my life committed enough to wake up early and stupid enough to think I would.

135 characters, one line. The turn: praise ("committed enough to wake up early")
flips into insult ("stupid enough to think I would") in the last three words.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python standard library only) reads `entry.txt`, validates the kit
gate in `check_gate()` (UTF-8, 1–200 characters, not only whitespace, single
line), prints the entry exactly, and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
